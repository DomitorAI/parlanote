# Parlanote privacy policy

Parlanote is made by DomitorAI. This policy covers every version of Parlanote: for Windows and for Android.

## What Parlanote collects

**Nothing.** Parlanote has no account, no telemetry, no analytics and no advertising. It has no server of its own
and does not send any information about you, your device or how you use it to the author or anyone else.

## What stays on your device

- **Your notebooks** (words, phrases, translations) and **your settings** — in the app's own storage on your device.
- They leave your device only when **you** export or share a file (**Back up / move to another device…**,
  **Share list…**), and only to where you send it.

On Windows they are kept when you uninstall Parlanote, unless you choose to delete them; on Android they are deleted
with the app.

## Connections

While you are online, and only for the word or phrase you are writing:

- **MyMemory** (translation suggestions, [mymemory.translated.net](https://mymemory.translated.net)) receives the
  word or phrase and its two languages. If you enter your e-mail in **Settings** (optional, for a higher daily
  limit), it is sent to MyMemory with each translation request. See the
  [MyMemory terms of use](https://mymemory.translated.net/terms-and-conditions).
- **LanguageTool** (spell check, [languagetool.org](https://languagetool.org)) receives the word or phrase and its
  language. You can turn the spell check off in **Settings**; then nothing is sent to LanguageTool. See the
  [LanguageTool privacy policy](https://languagetool.org/legal/privacy).

Once a day (if **Check for new versions** is on in **Settings**, the default):

- **GitHub** (`api.github.com`) receives a request for the number of the latest Parlanote version. The request
  contains no information about you. Nothing is downloaded until you choose to: when you press **Update**,
  Parlanote downloads the new version from the
  [Parlanote releases on GitHub](https://github.com/DomitorAI/parlanote/releases) and checks it before
  installing it. See the
  [GitHub privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

When you choose **Suggest a feature** or **Report a problem**, your browser opens a form on GitHub; what you write
there is public and covered by GitHub's terms.

Like any web request, these services see your IP address. Parlanote makes no other connections.

The voice comes from your device (Windows or Android text-to-speech), not from the internet.

## Children

Parlanote can be used in schools. It asks for no personal data, has no account and shows no ads.

## Contact

do not write personal details there).
